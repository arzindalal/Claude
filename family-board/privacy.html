Family Board privacy

Family Board is a private, non-commercial family organizer.

What the board stores

Everything a family adds to its board: names and emoji avatars, chores, events, routines, meals, lists, rewards, countdowns, photos, and settings. It is stored in Google Firebase and is readable and editable by anyone who has that family's private link.

Google Calendar

  • Connecting is optional, and each person chooses which of their calendars to share.
  • The board asks Google only for read-only access to calendars (calendar.readonly). It cannot create, change, or delete calendar events.
  • It copies the title, date, and time of events from the chosen calendars onto the family board. It does not copy descriptions, locations, attendees, or attachments.
  • The Google access token stays on the person's own device for about an hour and is never stored in the database. The board never sees passwords.
  • Disconnecting removes that person's calendar events from the board. Access can also be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Family Board's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Sharing

Data is not sold, shared with advertisers, or used for anything other than showing it on the family's own board.

Deleting data

Items can be deleted from the board at any time. To remove a whole family's data, contact the board owner.
